UAE Gratuity Calculator

By Muhammed Hadhil · Private sector · Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 · Last checked 13 Nov 2026

Work out end-of-service gratuity in seconds, with a full breakdown you can check line by line.

How the calculation works

  • Daily wage = last basic monthly salary ÷ 30
  • First 5 years: 21 days' basic wage for each year
  • After 5 years: 30 days' basic wage for each additional year
  • Part years are paid pro-rata, once 1 full year of service is completed
  • Cap: total gratuity cannot exceed 2 years' basic wages
  • Unpaid leave is deducted from the service period

Under the current law, gratuity is calculated the same way whether the employee resigns or is terminated. For the full rules and a worked example, read How End-of-Service Gratuity Is Calculated in the UAE.

Good to know: this calculator covers private-sector employees under the UAE Labour Law. It does not apply to free zones with their own rules (such as DIFC and ADGM), government employees, or employers using an approved alternative end-of-service savings scheme. The employer may also deduct amounts the employee owes.

This calculator gives an estimate for information only. It is not legal advice. Final amounts depend on the employment contract and the employer's records. For a specific case, check with MOHRE or a qualified adviser.
