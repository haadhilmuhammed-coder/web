The New UAE WPS Salary Rule: What Changed from 1 June 2026

By Muhammed Hadhil · Published 22 Oct 2026 · Last checked 22 Oct 2026 · 5 min read

Since 1 June 2026, UAE private-sector salaries must be paid through WPS before the first day of the following month. Here is what that means for payroll teams.

The Wage Protection System (WPS) is the electronic system the UAE uses to make sure private-sector employees are paid on time. Under Ministerial Resolution No. 340 of 2026 from the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation (MOHRE), the rules became stricter from 1 June 2026.

What the rule says

PointRule
DeadlineEach month's salaries must be transferred through WPS before the 1st day of the following month
Employer complianceAn employer counts as compliant when at least 85% of total wages are paid on time
Employee paidAn employee counts as paid when they receive at least 85% of their entitled salary, with any shortfall supported by documented legal deductions
ExemptionsIncludes employees in active disputes, employees reported absent, approved unpaid leave notified to MOHRE, and short-term permits under 3 months

What happens if salaries are late

According to published guidance, enforcement escalates the longer salaries stay unpaid:

  • Possible suspension of new work permits and formal violation notices
  • Administrative fines for repeated violations
  • Labour disputes registered and further permit suspensions
  • Stronger measures for long delays

A monthly payroll checklist

  1. Lock attendance and overtime early. Agree a cut-off date with site and HR teams
  2. Update the staff master. New joiners, leavers, salary changes, and active staff only
  3. Calculate and review payroll. Compare to last month and explain big changes
  4. Document every deduction. Advances, fines and loans need written support
  5. Prepare the WPS file. Check employee IDs and bank details match the system
  6. Upload days before the deadline, not on the last day, to leave time for bank rejections
  7. Reconcile after payment. Clear the salary control account and follow up rejected lines

On-time salary isn't just compliance. It's trust. Employees plan their lives around payday, and a reliable payroll is one of the clearest signs of a well-run finance team.
