How the calculation works

  • After 1 year of service: 30 calendar days of annual leave for each year
  • Between 6 months and 1 year: 2 days for each completed month
  • Under 6 months: no annual leave entitlement yet
  • Part years earn leave pro-rata when employment ends
  • Unused leave at end of service is paid on basic salary only: daily rate = basic monthly salary ÷ 30

Leave balance = leave earned − leave already taken. Payment = leave balance × daily rate. For end-of-service gratuity, use the UAE Gratuity Calculator.

Good to know: this calculator covers unused annual leave paid at the end of employment for private-sector employees. Pay for leave that is actually taken during employment follows the employment contract and company policy, and may include allowances. It does not apply to free zones with their own rules (such as DIFC and ADGM) or to government employees.

This calculator gives an estimate for information only. It is not legal advice. Final amounts depend on the employment contract and the employer's leave records. For a specific case, check with MOHRE or a qualified adviser.
